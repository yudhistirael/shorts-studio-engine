# Production

The skill orchestrates work across tools available in the host. Select sources, voice, and renderer per project. Rendering and voice generation require their own tools and credentials.

- Research and writing: source notes, question, script, and a shot list with visual purpose, timing, and start/end states.
- Voice: use an authorized provider, preserve delivery, and sync captions to actual audio. Reconcile ambiguous paid submissions before retrying. Keep credentials outside the project.
- Visuals: use a working Canvas/SVG, Remotion, Three.js, or Blender pipeline as appropriate. Inspect a real motion test at normal speed and phone size. Reuse rendering and compositing infrastructure across episodes.
- Delivery: start at 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264/AAC. Confirm video decodes, audio is audible, the title frame matches the thumbnail, and the last line and action finish cleanly.
- Publication: record render settings, QA findings, source references, metadata and any confirmed video ID. Keep drafts and release candidates distinguishable.
